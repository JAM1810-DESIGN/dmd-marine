You are continuing development of the **DMD Marine Consultation & Services** platform.

## Objective

Expand the Admin Dashboard by adding a comprehensive **Finance Module** that allows administrators to record business expenses, monitor income, generate financial reports, and view financial statements.

This module should integrate with the existing Booking, CRM, Projects, and Services modules so management can monitor the company's financial performance in one place.

The Finance Module must be scalable and designed to support future accounting features.
